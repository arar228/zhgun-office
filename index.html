Этот сайт создан в честь стикеров The Office от знаменитого художника Егора Жгуна.

The Office is a Telegram sticker pack created by the famous artist Egor Zhgun. It captures the chaotic, funny, and painfully relatable world of office life — endless meetings, deadlines, coffee addiction, awkward work chats, and corporate survival.

Each sticker represents a classic office emotion or situation, turning everyday workplace struggles into humor. The pack became a universal language for people who work in offices, remotely, or anywhere between burnout and “just one more call.”
